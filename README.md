# Hi, I'm Ruowen.

I work at the intersection of **strategy, operations, and technology**.

I enjoy the part of a project where the problem is still messy: understanding what people need, finding the right questions, making trade-offs explicit, and turning an idea into something practical. Depending on the challenge, that might mean a decision framework, a better workflow, a prototype, or a working digital tool.

I'm based in the Netherlands and hold an MSc in Metropolitan Analysis, Design and Engineering (TU Delft & Wageningen University).

## Beyond the code

Not every useful solution needs to be an app. My work also includes:

- **Emergency coordination:** helping develop task workflows, responsibility structures, and digital coordination processes in an NGO disaster-response context.
- **Business operations:** building internal tracking processes, dashboards, and team coordination routines while working in a small multidisciplinary venture.
- **Network and urban systems analysis:** using data, modelling, and decision frameworks to compare options and understand system-level trade-offs.

These experiences shape how I approach digital products: the technology should serve a real decision, user need, or operational process.

## How I work

**Understand the context → Structure the problem → Compare options → Build or improve the solution → Learn from use**

I try to balance analytical clarity with practical delivery. I am comfortable moving between stakeholder conversations, data, interface design, workflows, and implementation.
